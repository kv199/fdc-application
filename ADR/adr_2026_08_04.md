# Architectural Decision Records — 2026-08-04

## 17:56 - Separate the HUD from co-driver

### Context
- The initial HUD implementation lived inside a checkout of co-driver, mixing two independent release cycles and repository responsibilities.

### Decision
- Maintain Forza Horizon 6 HUD as a standalone private repository.
- Consume co-driver through its local WebSocket contract instead of modifying or embedding its source.
- Keep `main` releasable and perform integration work on `develop` in both local projects.

### Consequences
- HUD and co-driver can evolve, test, and release independently.
- Changes to the WebSocket contract must be coordinated between the projects.
- The original combined history remains available in `archive/combined-initial` for rollback.
