HOW TO READ DIAGNOSTICS
OBSERVED
Fallback target learned from your real upshifts.
OPTIMAL
Power crossover is stable and the gearbox ratio is validated.
LEARNING
The car or gear has not collected enough usable evidence.
WAITING FOR WOT
The power curve needs more full-throttle RPM coverage.
WAITING FOR RATIO
The current and next gear need more driven-wheel ratio samples.
CONFIRMING
A target was found and is being checked for stability.
GEARBOX MISMATCH
The live RPM drop does not match the stored gearbox evidence.

Power is measured at the target RPM before the shift and at the predicted RPM after the shift. The target is not simply the engine's peak-power RPM.
