HOW TO READ DIAGNOSTICS
LEARNING
The usable redline is the safe shift target. FDC lights the purple cue early enough to reach it while it watches normal full-throttle upshifts.
POTENTIAL
A comparable shift showed that the next gear was at least as powerful. Two more accepted shifts within 100 RPM confirm the candidate.
OPTIMAL
Three comparable shifts confirmed an earlier target. FDC uses their median RPM.

FDC compares engine power immediately before and after each usable next-gear shift. A negative result simply leaves the safe redline target in place. Incomplete, partial-throttle, or torque-cut captures are ignored without judging the shift.
