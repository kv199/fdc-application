## Forza Horizon 6 vehicle catalog

`src-tauri/data/vehicle-catalog.json` maps Forza Horizon 6 car ordinals to car
names and car types. It is generated by `tools/update-vehicle-catalog.mjs`,
with manual matches kept in `tools/vehicle-catalog-overrides.json`, from two
sources:

- **Forza Horizon 6 Car Ordinals** by HDR:
  <https://gist.github.com/HDR/0659d1717bc61504bf83750628963f4f>. The car
  ordinal list comes from this community-maintained gist. Thank you, HDR, for
  maintaining it and for allowing its use in open-source projects with
  attribution.
- **Official Forza Horizon 6 car list**: <https://forza.net/fh6cars>. Official
  car names and car types come from this list, © Microsoft Corporation.
